# Visual credits

`permian-environments.svg` and `extinction-mechanism.svg` are original explanatory diagrams created for Natural History Explorer, dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The Permian diagram is a schematic of contrasting habitats, not a paleogeographic reconstruction. Its scientific basis is the [National Park Service Permian overview](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/permian-period.htm).

The extinction diagram is a conceptual mechanism, not a quantitative model or an exhaustive account of extinction causes. Read clockwise from upper left: magmatism → gas release → warming and oxygen loss → less habitable ocean. Its scientific basis is [Burgess et al. (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-00083-9) and [Penn et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aat1327).

The header's geometric motif and favicon are original decorative artwork, not fossil specimens or scientific reconstructions. No external fonts or runtime image services are used.
